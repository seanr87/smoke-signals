Key terms

  • Smoke damage: main-gun shells fired from or through a smoke by the smoking team, plus the fires they started; each shot is credited to one smoke. Concealed damage is the part fired while the shooter was undetected; detected damage is the rest.
  • Concealed smoke damage: the part of the smoke damage fired while the shooter was undetected. Detected damage is the rest, broken down by how the shooter was spotted (radar, surface lit, hydro, proximity, aircraft, smoke fire penalty).
  • DD damage: smoke damage, concealed and detected, dealt to enemy destroyers; same credit rules as smoke damage.
  • Selfish smoke: no teammate other than the owner was ever inside it, and no teammate fired through it from behind. A tendency, not a flaw: some ships (Gdansk, for one) smoke selfishly by design. How often a clan or player smokes selfishly is a style signal.
  • Radar bait: a smoke where enemy radar spotted our ships inside it. Possibly a mistake: smokes are often laid knowing radar is a risk, and one spot is not a failure, but a high rate of radar-punished smokes for a clan or player is a warning sign.

A cap is contested for a team when it sits on the midline between the two spawns, lies on the enemy's side of the midline, or is held by the enemy.
