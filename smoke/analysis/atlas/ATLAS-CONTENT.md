# Smoke Atlas: what holds on every map

King of the Sea XIX NA. Where teams laid smoke, who stood in it, and what those smokes returned. Top 6 against the Rest.

1. The opening smoke is cruiser cover laid short of the cap: 14% inside a cap, typically 1.8 km outside (n 187). Example: Sleeping Giant G1.
2. Top 6 put a radar cruiser inside the early smoke: 60% (61 of 102) vs Rest 35% (70 of 200). Clearest at Hotspot G10.
3. Caps go with a cruiser in the smoke, not the radar press: taken 74% (n 53) unpressed, 58% (n 50) pressed. See Hotspot D.
4. Top 6 run radar from smoke 2.5 times as often: 0.47 vs 0.19 per team-game (52, 44 plays). Same return per play. Hotspot G10.
5. Deaths follow being behind and enemy radar, not distance: 23.1 per 100 smokes when 50+ behind (n 277), 25.0 under radar (n 120). Highest: Okinawa.
6. Somers and Gearing return alike (14.5k vs 16.3k; n 260, 180; gap not reliable). Somers deaths: 10.4 vs 17.8 per 100. See Sleeping Giant east.
7. Reflex counter-smokes return less: 8.0k vs 11.2k per destroyer smoke (n 133 counters). Not reliable within one habit. Most common on Land of Fire.
8. A radar cruiser sits inside 47% of all Top 6 destroyer smokes (n 387) vs 29% (n 687). Land of Fire Svea parks alone.

Fine print for the rules. Rule 1: early smokes by Harugumo, Somers and Gearing; the cruiser sits about 950 m on the spawn side of the smoke centre. Rule 2: gap +25 points (+13 to +36); Sleeping Giant is the one map where Top 6 are not ahead (47% vs 59%). Rule 3: cap-contest smokes with no radar ship inside, 32%. Rule 4: gap +0.28 (+0.14 to +0.40); cap taken after 58% of cap-control smokes for both groups (24 and 26 smokes). Rule 5: 6.5 per 100 when 50+ ahead (n 372), 9.9 without enemy radar (n 954); inside the cap 12.1, 3 to 5 km out 10.7. Rule 6: damage gap -1.8k (-5.4k to +3.4k); deaths gap -7.4 (-16.5 to -0.6). Rule 7: gap -5.5k to -0.9k; inside flank smoke lines 13.7k vs 16.0k (-7.2k to +2.1k). Rule 8: gap +18 points (+8 to +28); with a second destroyer forward as well, 16% vs 9% (+3 to +12).

## Page 9: Clan styles

# Two ways the Top 6 use smoke

**Long smoke, early, for a teammate: H-SR, RESIN, BONKZ** (305 smokes, 67 games). 61% of smokes from flank-smoke-line destroyers, 40% stacked on a teammate, 27% selfish, 20% stationary, first smoke at 2:43.

**Cruiser smoke, selfish, stationary: HTDOG, VRTUS** (188 smokes, 29 games). 45% of smokes from Svea or Jinan, 20% stacked, 51% selfish, 45% stationary, first smoke at 3:50. All gaps clear of zero.

**Supporting cast.** Both bring 3.3 destroyers per game; cruisers carrying smoke are 0.3 against 1.2. Cruisers deal most smoke damage in both (73%, 82%); the smoker deals 23% against 65%. A second destroyer is forward of a destroyer smoke in 29% (n 252) against 18% (n 92), not settled (-1 to +22).

**Conclusions.** Two ends of one scale. H-SR is the far end (71% flank lines, 50% stacked, first smoke 2:00). KITE (45 smokes) sits nearer the long-smoke style.

**Map captions.**
- Sleeping Giant: long smoke works both flanks (39% west, 46% east; 80 smokes); cruiser smoke stays west and centre (59% west, 8% east; 51).
- Hotspot: long smoke uses the east edge, column 10 (21 of 71). Cruiser-smoke clans: 14 smokes, too few.
- Mountain Range: 74% destroyer smoke in the middle third against 23%, spread west (50 and 26 smokes).
- Land of Fire: both smoke cap B; the cruiser style parks there (60% stationary against 17%; 16 of 40 in F5).

**PN** (14 smokes, 10 destroyer; small n): nearest the long-smoke style; no destroyer smoke in a signature spot.

## Sidebar: Ship habits

**Four habits, set by the ship.**
- Flank smoke lines (Somers, Gearing, Harugumo, Delny; n 562): laid moving, 55% stacked, 15.8k per smoke. Hotspot G10.
- Cap sitters (Daring, Z-42, Gdańsk, Grozovoi, Shimakaze; n 462): 50% stationary, 37% inside a cap, 5.3k. Hotspot cap C.
- Parked cruiser smoke (Svea, Jinan; n 241): 67% stationary, 8.4k. Land of Fire F5.
- Moving exhaust (Napoli, Venezia, C. Colombo; n 188): 4.1k, 17 deaths per 100.

**Grozovoi and Shimakaze are a mix, not two clean modes.** Smokes that look like a moving line: Shimakaze 64% (52 to 78, n 58), Grozovoi 53% (34 to 83, n 30, half one clan), Gdańsk 41%, Daring 10%. The same players do both.

## Footer

Data: King of the Sea XIX NA replays, batch output/20261117-122135 (1,547 smokes, 171 games). Concealed damage is damage dealt while hidden by the smoke, torpedoes launched from smoke included. Top 6 = H-SR (20-2), RESIN (17-7), BONKZ (13-8), HTDOG (12-6), KITE (9-5), VRTUS (7-4); Rest = everyone else. Maps are drawn with own spawn at the bottom (bottom-left on Okinawa). Ship positions exist only at the moment each smoke was fully laid.

Association, not cause: this atlas describes where teams smoked and what those smokes returned; it does not show that any spot or habit produces a result.

Small-n rule: no spot, cell or cluster is reported below 8 smokes; 8 to 14 is marked small n. Counts inside a spot can be smaller. Intervals are 95%, made by resampling clans.

Placebo caveat: six Rest clans standing in as the "top" group pass 2.5 signature spots on average, so 2 to 3 of the signature spots are the kind any group of six clans shows.
