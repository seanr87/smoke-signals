Batch `output/20261117-122135`. Positions are team-normalized: own spawn at the bottom; north-spawn teams are mirrored north-south, and on Okinawa across the NW-SE diagonal (own spawn bottom-left). Everything here is association: where Top 6 smoke and what those smokes returned, not what causes a win.

## Findings

- **6 signature spots and 5 leads**, from 24 candidate spots tested (43 ship-or-habit x map combinations had at least 8 Top 6 smokes). 8 candidates passed the rule; 2 were the same place seen at both ship and habit level.
- **Read these as 'where the Top 6 go', not as proven differences.** A placebo run (the same search with six busy Rest clans standing in as the 'top' group, 20 draws) passed 2.5 spots on average (range 0 to 5) out of 9 candidates. So any six clans show a few favourite spots; the Top 6 show 8, more than any placebo draw, but roughly 2 or 3 of them are the kind any group would produce. Best supported as tight spots: 2 (Land of Fire Svea) and 5 (Sleeping Giant Harugumo). Spots 1 and 3 are clear but are wide zones. Weakest: 4 (two clans, and mostly a ship the Rest did not bring) and 6 (interval barely clear of zero).
- **The one cast pattern that holds across maps is a radar cruiser inside the destroyer smoke**: 47% of Top 6 destroyer smokes vs 29% of the Rest (gap +18 pts, +8 to +28). Adding 'second destroyer forward' makes it rare: 16% vs 9%.
- **Sleeping Giant Harugumo at G1/F1**: a radar cruiser is inside in 9 of 11 (Des Moines or Worcester, not Brisbane); a second destroyer is recorded in 7 of 11.

## Method and cautions

- **Search.** Every ship and every habit cluster (flank smoke lines, cap sitters, parked cruiser smoke, moving exhaust) on every map, destroyers and cruisers, with at least 8 Top 6 smokes: 43 combinations. Top 6 smoke centres were clustered by mean-shift (Gaussian kernel, 2.0 km bandwidth; density peaks within 1 km merged): 275 clusters, most of them one or two smokes. A spot's region is the 2-standard-deviation ellipse of its cluster (each radius at least 1 km). Any ellipse holding at least 8 Top 6 smokes is a candidate: 24.
- **Test.** Share of the Top 6 smokes of that ship or habit on that map that fall inside the ellipse, minus the same share for the Rest. 95% interval by resampling clans, 1000 times. Kept if at least 8 Top 6 smokes, at least 2 Top 6 clans, the Rest have at least 8 smokes of that subject on the map, and the interval is above zero.
- **The test flatters the Top 6.** The ellipse is drawn around Top 6 smokes and then used to count them, and clans simply have habits. That is what the placebo measures: 2.5 passes per draw with no real Top 6 in it. With 2 to 4 Top 6 clans behind each spot the clan resampling also has little to work with (the Mountain Range interval of +31 to +40 is narrow only because no Rest smoke is there at all).
- **Scale matters.** At 1.5 km: 13 candidates, 5 pass. At 2.5 km: 24 candidates, 8 pass. Each spot lists how it fares at the other two scales. Spots 1 and 3 only appear at 2 km and wider and are zones 17 to 20 km long, not tight spots.
- **Cast positions come from one moment only**: when each smoke was fully laid. Not at activation and not continuously. On top of that, the batch has a position at that moment for only about 4 allied ships per smoke (median), so "no second destroyer recorded" does not mean there was none, and the nearest-ally stand-in picks from the ships that have a position.
- **Spotting cannot be attributed from the batch.** "Nearest allied ship to the target" is a stand-in. Median distances of 8 to 9 km mean the nearest ally is often outside destroyer detection range, so treat it as "who was closest", not "who spotted".
- **Inside / behind / outside** is the batch's own label for each ally over the smoke's life; line of fire is counted as outside. Distances are to the nearest puff centre at the laid moment.
- Concealed damage includes torpedoes launched from smoke. Cap won is counted only on smokes whose contested cap was not already ours. Mountain Range cap A (10 km) is kept.
- `ellipse_uv.angle_deg` in `spots.json` is the rotation of the `ru` axis clockwise on screen from the u (east) axis, as used by SVG `rotate`.
- Files: `spotlib.py` (search and test), `explore_candidates.py` (scales and placebo, writes `checks.json`), `spots_build.py` + `spots_report.py` (measures, figures, this file, `spots.json`). Figures in `fig/`.
