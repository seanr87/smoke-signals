Maps

Each map shows a density of smoke origins (gold squares, darker where more smokes started) for the smokes the filters leave; when 50 or fewer remain, the smokes themselves are drawn: the cloud, the path while laying, and an arrow at the end, in the clan's colour. Click a drawn smoke to open its page. The View switch forces either picture. Filters apply to every map and are kept in the address bar, so a view can be shared.
